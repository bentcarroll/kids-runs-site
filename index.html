Welland kids' runs

A private household tool that works out who drives to school and activities, and keeps the family informed by email.

What it does

  • Reads the household's own shared calendars and works out, week by week, which parent can drive each school and activity run.
  • Emails the household's parents when a week's plan breaks, for example when a run has no driver.
  • Sends relatives a short weekly email about the coming week's rides.

Who uses it

Welland kids' runs runs on a computer in one family's home and is used by that one household. It is not a public service. It has no sign-up, no user accounts and no advertising, and nobody outside the household can use it.

The Google permission it asks for

The emails go out from one dedicated household Gmail account. To send them, the app asks that account for a single Google permission: send email on its behalf (gmail.send). With it the app cannot read, search, list or delete any mail, and it cannot see contacts, calendar or files through this sign-in.

Privacy policy: read how the app handles data. In short, nothing is sold or shared, and the sign-in is kept on one private computer.
