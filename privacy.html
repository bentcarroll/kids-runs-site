Welland kids' runs

Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Welland kids' runs is a private tool that one household runs for its own use. In this policy, "the owner" means the person who runs it for that household.

In short: the app asks for one Google permission, to send email from one dedicated household account. It cannot read any mail. The sign-in is stored on one private computer in the owner's home. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

What Google data the app accesses

The app uses Google sign-in for one account only, the household's dedicated sending account. It asks for exactly one permission, "send email on your behalf" (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send).

The only Google data the app receives from that sign-in is the authorisation itself: an access token and a refresh token for that one send permission. The app does not ask for, and cannot use, any permission to read, search, list, label or delete messages, or to see contacts, calendar or files through this sign-in.

How the app uses it

Only to send the household's ride-planning emails: a note to the household's parents when a week's plan breaks, and a short weekly note to relatives about the coming week's rides. They go to a short fixed list of addresses that the owner has entered in the app's private settings. The emails contain ride schedules: which runs there are and who is driving. The app sends nothing else, and nothing to anyone outside that list.

Calendars

Separately from this sign-in, the app reads and writes the household's own calendars, which the household's members have shared with a Google service account that the owner controls. That is not Google sign-in data from anyone outside the household.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

The tokens and the app's Google credentials are kept in a private file on one computer in the owner's home. Only the program's own system account and the computer's administrator (the owner) can read the file. They are not uploaded to any other service. The app also keeps a small local record of each email it sent (the date and whether it was delivered), so that it never sends the same note twice. No analytics or tracking is used, here or on this website.

Sharing, selling and other uses

The owner does not sell, rent or share this data, and the app does not use it for advertising, profiling or training any model. The only other parties that handle the emails are Google, which delivers them, and the recipients' own mail providers. No person other than the owner reads the app's data.

Welland kids' runs' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

The sign-in is kept for as long as the household uses the app. It can be ended at any time: the sending account can remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the owner can delete the stored tokens, which ends the app's ability to send. To ask for anything the app holds to be deleted, write to the address below.
